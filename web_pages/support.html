Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign up as a driver?

Download the Sam's Deli Driver app, tap "Sign Up," fill in your details, and upload your selfie, driver's license, and insurance document. Your application will be reviewed and approved by our team.

How long does approval take?

Driver applications are typically reviewed within 1–2 business days. You'll be able to start accepting orders once approved.

How do I get paid?

Your earnings accumulate as you complete deliveries. You can request a cashout from the Profile screen when your balance reaches the minimum threshold. Cashouts are processed within 1–3 business days.

Why do I need to take photos at pickup and delivery?

Photos serve as proof of pickup and delivery, protecting both you and the customer. They help resolve any disputes about order fulfillment.

Why does the app need my location?

Location is used to match you with nearby orders, provide navigation, and verify that you are at the store or customer's address before confirming pickup or delivery.

How do I go offline?

Tap the Online/Offline toggle at the top of the home screen. When offline, you won't receive new order requests.

How do I delete my account?

To request account deletion, please email us at samsdeliandmarket@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and your registered email address. We will process your request within 5 business days.

I'm having trouble with the app. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app. Make sure you have a stable internet connection and location services enabled. If the issue persists, contact us with a description of the problem.
