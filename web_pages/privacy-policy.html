Privacy Policy — Sam's Deli Driver App

Last Updated: May 9, 2026

Sam's Deli & Market ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Sam's Deli Driver mobile application ("Driver App").

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information Provided by You

When you register and use the Driver App, we collect:

  • Full Name: For identification and display on delivery orders
  • Email Address: For account authentication and communications
  • Phone Number: For contact related to deliveries and account verification
  • Password: Securely stored for account access
  • Face Photo (Selfie): A camera-captured selfie taken during registration, used for identity verification
  • Driver's License: Photo of your driver's license for verification purposes
  • Insurance Document: Photo of your insurance document for compliance
  • Payment Information: Bank account or payment method details for cashout/payout purposes

Information Collected During Use

  • Location Data: Real-time GPS location while online and during active deliveries, used for navigation, distance calculation, and delivery verification
  • Pickup Photos: Photos taken at the store to confirm order pickup
  • Delivery Photos: Photos taken at the customer's door to confirm delivery completion
  • Delivery History: Records of completed and cancelled deliveries
  • Earnings Data: Delivery fees, tips, bonuses, and cashout history

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, online/offline status, time spent in app
  • Push Notification Tokens: For sending order alerts and delivery updates

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Verify your identity and approve your driver account
  • Assign and manage delivery orders
  • Track deliveries in real-time for customer visibility
  • Verify pickup and delivery with location checks and photo proof
  • Calculate and process your earnings and cashout requests
  • Send push notifications for new orders and delivery updates
  • Provide navigation and routing to pickup and delivery locations
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform safety
  • Improve our services and driver experience
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Location Data

The Driver App collects your precise location:

  • When Online: To match you with nearby delivery orders
  • During Active Deliveries: To provide real-time tracking, navigation, and verify you are at the pickup/delivery location before allowing confirmation
  • Background Location: When you are online and the app is in the background, to maintain delivery tracking accuracy

You can stop location collection by going offline in the app or closing the app entirely. Location sharing is required to accept and complete deliveries.

4. Photo Data

The Driver App requires camera access for:

  • Registration Selfie: A front-camera photo of your face, required during sign-up for identity verification. Gallery selection is not permitted — the photo must be taken live.
  • Pickup Confirmation: A photo of the order at the store, required before confirming pickup
  • Delivery Confirmation: A photo of the delivered order at the customer's location, required before confirming delivery
  • Document Uploads: Photos of your driver's license and insurance during registration

All photos are stored securely in Firebase Cloud Storage and are accessible only to authorized personnel.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Customers: Your first name and general location during active deliveries for tracking purposes
  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google) for authentication, data storage, and cloud storage; Google Maps for navigation
  • Store Partners: Order-related information necessary for pickup coordination
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • All data is transmitted using SSL/TLS encryption
  • Authentication is handled securely through Firebase Authentication
  • Photos and documents are stored in encrypted Firebase Cloud Storage
  • Firestore database is protected with security rules
  • Payment information is handled with industry-standard security practices

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Update your information through the app's profile settings
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us
  • Location: Go offline to stop location tracking; disable location permissions in device settings
  • Notifications: Manage push notification preferences in your device settings

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Maintain your active driver account
  • Process pending earnings and cashout requests
  • Comply with legal and tax obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

Delivery photos are retained for a reasonable period for dispute resolution. You may request account deletion by contacting us.

9. Children's Privacy

The Driver App is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.

10. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging
  • Google Maps: Navigation and location services
  • Apple Maps: Alternative navigation option

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Driver App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected and how it is used
  • Right to request deletion of personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights
  • We do not sell personal information
